Our paper, [“Biased Backpressure Routing for Multihop Wireless Networks with Heterogeneous Interfaces”](/publications/2026-09-01-multi-layer-sp-bp-milcom26.html), has been accepted to IEEE [MILCOM 2026](https://milcom2026.ieee-milcom.org/)!

Congratulations to [**Yujun Ming**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yujun-ming-684078267/), who led this work and will present it at the conference’s **Young Scholar Workshop** (YSW) poster session. This is the first paper produced under a research project for which I serve as PI.

See you in Washington, D.C., October 12–16!
