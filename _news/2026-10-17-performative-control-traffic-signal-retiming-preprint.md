I am pleased to share our preprint, [“A Performative Control Approach to Day-to-Day Traffic Signal Retiming”](/publications/2026-10-13-performative-control-traffic-signal-retiming.html), now available on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19022)! This is the **first research paper by lead author Shuchang Jiang, an undergraduate student at Rice University**.

Traffic signal control does more than manage congestion: it also influences how travelers choose routes and whether they travel at all. We propose a scalable, gradient-based approach to day-to-day retiming of fixed-time traffic signals that uses traffic observations and offline simulations without requiring an explicit model of travelers' behavioral responses. Incremental adjustments allow signal policies and travel demand to evolve together toward a stable operating point, a concept known as *performative stability*.

This collaboration with **Xinwu Qian**, of **Rice University's Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering**, draws on his **expertise in transportation systems and performative control**. Together, we extend methodologies developed in **Santiago Segarra's research group** for intelligent wireless networking to transportation networks.
